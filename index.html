Formats

A different team game each day, with Net Skins and Closest to the Pin running alongside.

Day 1 · Friday

Best Ball

Best 2 of the four. On every hole the team's two best net scores count, added together. Add up all 18 holes; the lowest team total wins the day.

One good swing from anyone on the team saves the hole.

Day 2 · Saturday

30/40 Ball

Each team has 36 net scores per nine (9 per player) and must count exactly 20 of them. After each hole, before teeing off on the next, the team picks which balls count: anywhere from none to all four.

Each counted ball is scored against par. Lowest team total wins. Spend your good balls wisely.

Day 3 · Sunday

Team Stableford

Points instead of strokes. All four players earn net points on every hole and the team adds them up. Most points wins.

Handicaps & tees

  • Everyone plays the II tees (6,626 yards, 71.4 / 130).
  • Handicap index from GHIN, locked on November 23.
  • 100% of course handicap in every game. Everything is net.

Net Skins · validated

  • Lowest net score on a hole, outright, wins the skin. A tie means no one wins that hole.
  • Validation: the winner must back it up with net par or better on the next hole. If not, that skin pays nothing and the next skin won is worth double. Everyone gets a notification when it happens.
  • No validation needed on 18. A skin is worth the day's pot divided by the skins won: with 4 skins won, $120 each. If the 2nd isn't backed up: $120, $0, $240, $120.

Closest to the Pin

  • One winner on each par 3, every day.
  • The day's $160 is shared equally over the par 3s that are won.
  • Ball must be on the green.

Teams

  • Four teams of four each day: one player from each flight, A to D.
  • Teams change daily. No two players are teammates twice.
  • Final teams are set once handicaps lock on November 23.

Scores in the app

  • Join in the B&B Golf app with invitation code WCDREI.
  • Enter scores hole by hole; games, skins and payouts update live.

Etiquette

  • Ready golf. Pick up when you're out of the hole.
  • Settle up Sunday. Complaints about the weather will not be heard.
